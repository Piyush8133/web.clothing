Store Policies

1. 7-Day Doorstep Exchange & Return Policy

Unworn garments with original tags intact can be exchanged for a different size or returned within 7 days of delivery. Free doorstep pickup is available across Dehradun municipal limits.

2. Same-Day Dehradun Shipping

Orders above ₹1,499 qualify for free shipping. Orders placed before 4:00 PM IST for Rajpur Road, Jakhan, Vasant Vihar, Clement Town, and Sahastradhara Road are delivered the same evening.

3. Privacy Policy

We collect your name, delivery address, and phone number strictly to fulfil orders and send dispatch updates. We never sell or rent customer data to third parties.

4. Terms & Conditions

All prices listed on Dehra Fashion Hub are in Indian Rupees (INR) and inclusive of applicable GST.
